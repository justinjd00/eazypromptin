**Fill in before sending:** the reminder send time, your country, your business name, and roughly how many jobs you do per week.

**Two things to know about the SMS part:**

1. **SMS is probably your biggest cost, not hosting.** Hosting an app like this costs your friend about €5–10/month. SMS in Europe typically costs a few cents to around 10 cents per message, depending on the provider and country. At 100 jobs a week that's roughly 430 messages a month, which on its own can come close to your €30. That's why the prompt asks for a cost estimate before any building starts. If it doesn't fit, the cheap fixes are reminders only for new or one-off clients, or email reminders, which are basically free.
2. **Consent.** In the EU you need clients' permission to text them. That's what the per-client yes/no setting is for. Before turning reminders on, send your regulars one message telling them about the reminders and how to opt out.

One gap worth thinking about: right now the WhatsApp group probably also handles "client cancelled, someone take over Thursday." This version doesn't notify cleaners when their schedule changes, so they'll need to check the app. If that's a big part of how you work today, tell me and I'll add a simple notification for cleaners when a job is assigned, moved, or cancelled.
