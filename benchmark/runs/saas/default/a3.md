Good addition. In Germany it matters more than you might think: commercial cleaning is one of the sectors where employers have to record start time, end time and duration of each employee's working day. Those records must be made within 7 days and kept for at least 2 years (§17 MiLoG together with §2a SchwarzArbG). So check-in/out isn't just a convenience. It can become your official time record, and the prompt treats it that way.

Here's what I decided for the open points:

- **Language:** German UI and German SMS. Everything else, like code and the README, in English or German as your friend prefers.
- **Reminder time:** 18:00 Europe/Berlin the day before.
- **Hosting:** EU-based (e.g. Hetzner). The agent proposes the exact setup.
- **SMS:** a provider with EU/German data processing and a data processing agreement (AVV). The agent compares prices.
- **Location check:** when a cleaner checks in or out, the app reads their location once and stores only the distance to the client's address, not the coordinates. If they're more than ~300 m away, the entry gets flagged for you. No tracking in between. This shows whether someone was really on site, with as little employee data as possible. Your cleaners need to be told about it in writing beforehand.
- **Travel time:** see the note at the end. The export includes it so you don't have to decide now.

> Build a simple web app for my cleaning business in Germany to replace the WhatsApp group and Excel sheet we currently use to manage jobs. A developer friend will run and host it, so pick a stack that one person can maintain easily. The UI and all SMS must be in German. Keep it simple and don't add features I didn't ask for.
>
> **Users**
> - **Owner (me):** full access.
> - **Cleaners (6):** each logs in on their phone and sees only their own jobs and their own time entries. They must not see other cleaners' jobs, the full client list, or prices. Enforce this on the server, not just in the UI.
>
> **Version 1 features**
> 1. **Clients:** add, edit and search clients: name, mobile number, email, address, access notes (key/gate codes, pets, alarm), default price, and an "SMS-Erinnerung: ja/nein" setting (default: no, set to yes only after consent).
> 2. **Excel import:** a one-time import of my existing client list from .xlsx/.csv, with a preview that flags rows with missing or invalid German mobile numbers before saving. Ask me for a sample file before building this step.
> 3. **Jobs:** date, start time, estimated duration, assigned cleaner(s), price, notes. Status: Geplant → Erledigt, or Storniert.
> 4. **Recurring jobs:** weekly, every 2 weeks, or monthly. I must be able to move, reassign or skip a single visit, and edit the series from a given date onward.
> 5. **Owner schedule:** day and week views of all jobs, filterable by cleaner, with a warning when a cleaner is double-booked.
> 6. **Cleaner view (mobile-first):** "Meine Einsätze" for today and the next few days, with the address (tap to open in Google Maps), access notes, job notes, and two large buttons: **Einchecken** on arrival and **Auschecken** on leaving, with an optional comment. Checking out marks the job as done.
> 7. **Time tracking:**
>    - Check-in and check-out store server timestamps, not phone time.
>    - On check-in/out, request the device location once, compute the distance to the client address, and store only that distance plus a flag if it's over 300 m. Never store raw coordinates and never track location in between. If location is denied or unavailable, still allow the check-in and flag it "Standort nicht verfügbar".
>    - Cleaners can't edit their own times. If they forget to check out, the entry is flagged as "offen" and shown to me. I can correct or add entries, but every correction is saved with the old value, the new value, who changed it, when, and a required reason. Original entries are never overwritten or deleted.
>    - Keep time records for at least 2 years.
> 8. **Monthly payroll export:** I pick a month and get a CSV file with one row per check-in/out: cleaner, date, client, check-in, check-out, on-site duration, flags. Then one summary row per cleaner per day with first check-in, last check-out, total on-site hours, and total daily span (first check-in to last check-out). Show durations both as decimal hours and hh:mm. Format for German Excel: semicolon-separated, comma as decimal separator, UTF-8 with BOM, dates as DD.MM.YYYY. Also provide a per-cleaner option. Before exporting, show me all open or flagged entries for that month so I can fix them first.
> 9. **SMS reminders:** every day at 18:00 Europe/Berlin, send an SMS to each client with a job the next day who has reminders turned on. Editable template, default: "Hallo [Vorname], zur Erinnerung: [Firmenname] kommt morgen um [Uhrzeit]. Zum Verschieben bitte [Telefon] anrufen." Skip cancelled jobs. Log every SMS (sent/failed) and show failures to me. Use an SMS provider with EU data processing and a DPA (AVV), and keep the provider swappable.
> 10. **Job report:** completed jobs per cleaner and total value for a chosen date range.
>
> **Not in version 1:** payments, invoicing, client self-booking, two-way SMS, a native app, direct payroll software integration.
>
> **Constraints**
> - Total running cost under €30/month, including hosting, database and SMS. Before coding, give me a cost estimate assuming about 80 jobs per week, plus your stack, hosting and SMS provider choices with a few sentences on why.
> - Host and store all data in the EU. HTTPS only, hashed passwords, automatic daily database backups kept for at least 30 days.
> - Secure login for all 7 users. I can reset a cleaner's password and deactivate a cleaner who leaves, while keeping their historical time records.

>

> - Ask before coding anywhere you'd otherwise have to guess.
> - Build in this order, with something working after each step: auth and roles → clients and import → jobs and recurring → owner schedule → cleaner view with check-in/out → corrections and payroll CSV → SMS reminders → job report.

On the day, only that cleaner sees the job and checks in and out on site. **Only one thing left to fill in:** your business name in the SMS template. If you do much more or fewer than ~80 jobs a week, change that number too, because it drives the SMS cost estimate.

**The travel time question:** if a cleaner does three clients in a day, the drive between them usually counts as working time in Germany. If you pay only on-site hours, you'll likely underpay. That's why the CSV has both the on-site total and the full daily span. Ask your Steuerberater or payroll office which one they need before the first payroll run. The same goes for breaks: the app doesn't record them separately, so they'd have to be deducted by a rule (e.g. 30 min after 6 hours) if needed. Tell me if you want that added.

**Before going live**, two short documents, not code: a written notice to your cleaners about the time tracking and the location check, and an AVV with your hosting and SMS providers. Both are usually templates you sign or adapt.
