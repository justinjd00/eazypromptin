"Manage the jobs" usually means one of these three things. These are just to find your direction. Pick one, mix them ("A plus the invoicing from B"), or tell me why none fits:

**A) Schedule board.** A calendar of jobs by day. You assign each job to a cleaner. The cleaner opens a link on their phone and sees today's addresses and notes (key code, pets, "don't use bleach in the bathroom"), then ticks each job off. Roughly a stripped-down Jobber or ZenMaid.
**B) Clients & billing log.** A list of clients with address, price and how often they're cleaned. Every completed job is logged. At the end of the month it shows what to invoice and what's still unpaid, and makes a simple invoice PDF.
**C) Booking front door.** Clients request a cleaning online (date, size of the home, an instant price). You approve it and it goes into your schedule.
**Wildcard: no custom app.** A properly set-up Google Sheet plus a form, or Airtable. You'd have it in an afternoon with nothing to host. Building only makes sense once you've outgrown that.

One honest note before we go on: there are ready-made tools for exactly this (Jobber, ZenMaid and similar, paid monthly). Building your own is worth it when those are too expensive, do too much, or don't fit how you work. The catch is that someone has to host it and fix it when it breaks. Question 3 is about that.

1. **Who uses it?** (decides logins and what each person can see)
   a) Me plus my cleaners, and each cleaner sees only their own jobs on their phone (recommended, it's usually the biggest daily pain)
   b) Just me
   c) Me, my cleaners, and my clients too
2. **How do your jobs usually come in?** (decides whether the app has to create repeating jobs automatically)
   a) Mostly regular clients, weekly or every two weeks
   b) Mostly one-off cleans (move-outs, deep cleans)
   c) A mix of both
3. **Who looks after it once it's built?** (decides how simple the setup and hosting need to be)
   a) Not me, I'm not technical, so it has to run without anyone touching code (recommended assumption unless you say otherwise)
   b) Me, I'm fairly comfortable with tech or code (say which, if any)
   c) Someone else handles tech for me
4. **What do you use today, and what annoys you about it?** e.g. paper and WhatsApp, a spreadsheet, Google Calendar, or another app. One line is plenty. The annoying part is usually what the tool should fix.
