Happy to help. "Invoices" can mean two pretty different problems, though, and the fix depends on which one is burying you:

**A) Invoices you send** (billing your clients)
The pain is usually creating them, sending them, and chasing late payers.
- Use an invoicing tool that does recurring invoices and automatic payment reminders: FreshBooks, Zoho Invoice (free tier), QuickBooks, Xero, or Stripe Invoicing if you already take card payments. In Germany, sevDesk or Lexoffice.
- Turn on "remind at 3 / 7 / 14 days overdue" and online payment links. That alone kills most of the chasing.
- If you bill from time tracking or a project tool, connect it (Toggl, Harvest, Clockify) so invoices draft themselves at month end.

**B) Invoices you receive** (bills from suppliers)
The pain is usually collecting PDFs from email, entering the data, filing them, and paying on time.
- Set up a dedicated inbox like bills@yourdomain and get every vendor to send there.
- Point that inbox at a tool that reads invoices automatically (OCR/AI extraction): Dext, Hubdoc, the built-in inbox in Xero/QuickBooks, or GetMyInvoices / Candis in Germany. It pulls out vendor, amount, due date, VAT, and pushes it into your accounting.
- DIY option if you like tinkering: a Zapier, Make or n8n flow that grabs attachments from Gmail, saves them to a Drive folder named by month, and logs vendor, amount and due date to a spreadsheet using an AI extraction step.
