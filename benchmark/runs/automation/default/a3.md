Got it. Here are both workflows ready to import. The decisions I made for you:

- **LLM: Mistral Small** via Mistral's API. It's an EU company with EU hosting, so invoice data stays in the EU, it's very good at pulling fields from German/French/etc. text, and at your volume (maybe 10 to 20 invoices a month) it costs well under 1 euro a month. Temperature 0 so it doesn't get creative with amounts.
- **Filename:** `2026-10-14_SupplierName_1234.56.pdf`. ISO date first so the folder sorts chronologically for your tax advisor. Amount is the gross (what you actually pay), with a dot, because commas in filenames cause trouble in some tools.
- **Supplier names come from a lookup table, not the LLM.** Otherwise one month you get `Telekom`, the next `Telekom-Deutschland-GmbH`. You fill in your 5 suppliers once in the `Combine` node.
- **One flat folder** in Drive, shared with your advisor. Only clean invoices land there. Anything that fails a check goes to Telegram and the `Invoices/Check` label instead, so your advisor never sees a misnamed file.

## Setup (about 20 minutes)

1. **Google Sheet:** create a sheet with a tab named `Invoices` and these headers in row 1:
   `invoice_date | due_date | supplier | invoice_number | net | vat | gross | file_name | file_link | status | processed_at`
   Put its ID (the long part of the URL) in place of `YOUR_SHEET_ID` in both workflows.
2. **Drive folder:** create it, share it with your tax advisor, put its ID in `YOUR_TAX_FOLDER_ID`.
3. **Telegram bot:** message @BotFather, `/newbot`, copy the token into a Telegram credential in n8n. Then send your bot any message and open `https://api.telegram.org/bot<TOKEN>/getUpdates` to find your chat ID. Put it in `YOUR_CHAT_ID`.
4. **Mistral:** create an API key at console.mistral.ai and add it as a Mistral credential.
5. **Gmail labels:** in the three Gmail nodes and the trigger, the label fields are placeholders. Click each one and pick the real label from the dropdown (`Invoices/New`, `Invoices/Done`, `Invoices/Check`).
6. **Credentials:** open each Gmail, Drive, Sheets, Telegram and Mistral node once and select your credential.
7. **Supplier table:** edit `SUPPLIERS` at the top of the `Combine` node with your 5 sender domains and the names you want in filenames.

## Test before you switch it on

- Apply `Invoices/New` to two or three old invoice emails, then hit **Test workflow**. Compare the sheet rows and filenames against the PDFs.
- `Skip resent invoices` remembers every invoice it has seen, including test runs. If you want to rerun the same test emails, clear its memory (the node has a "clear deduplication history" option) or temporarily disable it.
- Once it looks right, backfill the last few months by labeling old emails, then activate both workflows.

## Two things to know

- **Scanned PDFs:** if a supplier sends a scan instead of a generated PDF, the text is empty and the invoice lands in review via Telegram. With 5 suppliers you'll know within a month whether that happens. If one does, tell me and I'll add a branch that sends that supplier's PDFs to Mistral's OCR instead.
- **Marking paid:** change `status` to `paid` in the sheet when you pay, otherwise the Monday message keeps listing it as overdue. That's the one manual step left. If your bank supports it, matching payments automatically is a possible next step, but I'd run this version for a month first.
