Done. Both changes are in, here's what changed and then the full updated workflows.

**Folder per month**
- Inside your tax advisor folder you now get `2026-09`, `2026-10`, `2026-11` and so on. The month comes from the **invoice date**, not the day the email arrived, so a September invoice that shows up on 2 October still goes into `2026-09`. That's what your advisor books it under.
- The workflow looks for the month folder and creates it if it doesn't exist yet. Invoices are now processed one at a time (`Loop Over Items`), otherwise two invoices from a new month arriving together would create two `2026-11` folders.
- Your advisor only needs access to the parent folder. New month folders inherit the sharing automatically.

**Flag over 500 EUR**
- Gross amount above 500 means an immediate Telegram message the moment it's processed: amount, supplier, invoice number, due date, link to the PDF.
- The sheet gets a new `flag` column, so you can filter for large invoices later.
- In the Monday summary, those lines are marked with `[!]`.
- Flagged invoices are still filed normally. The flag is a heads-up, not a hold. The threshold is one number at the top of the `Combine` node if you want to change it.
