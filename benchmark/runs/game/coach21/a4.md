# Dungeon Buddies (working title): Brief

> ✅ decided by you · 🔶 assumed (default, veto anytime) · ❓ open

## 1. Vision & why
Two friends in different cities team up online as two cute little heroes, dive through a bright, goofy pixel-art dungeon, grab an upgrade after every room and try to beat the boss in one 15-20 minute run. Losing stings a little, but you come back slightly stronger. ✅
Made for the author and one friend to actually play on game nights; a Steam release is "maybe someday". ✅
Reference anchors:
- Soul Knight: cute pixel look, fast shooting, short co-op runs.
- Enter the Gungeon: WASD + mouse aim + dodge-roll feel.
- Hades: pick 1 of 3 upgrades after each room.
Anti-goals: ✅ no grinding (the author hates it) · ✅ not dark or gory · no story or dialogue · no 3+ player support.

## 2. Audience
Exactly 2 players, both on PC with keyboard + mouse, in different cities, playing online. ✅

## 3. Scope
**Version 1:**
- ✅ Online co-op, exactly 2 players (host + 1 client)
- ✅ Top-down twin-stick shooting: WASD move, mouse aim, click to shoot, dodge-roll
- ✅ 2 heroes with different starting weapons: pistol hero and shotgun hero
- 🔶 1 dungeon area (classic fantasy, goofy), 6-8 hand-made room layouts, rooms 1-6 drawn randomly from the pool, then 1 boss room

## 4. Experience

### Core loop
Room → doors lock → clear the monsters → each player picks 1 of 3 upgrades → both walk to the exit → next room → … → boss → camp → spend coins → new run.
- 30 seconds: dodge, aim, shoot, revive your buddy.
- 5 minutes: 2-3 rooms, a build starting to take shape.
- Session: one 15-20 min run, then "one more?".

### First two minutes
1. Player 1 clicks Host, player 2 clicks Join and enters the host's address.
2. Each picks a hero (pistol or shotgun; both can pick the same one).
3. Both spawn in a small bright start room with the first exit open.
4. Room 1: doors lock, ~6 slimes pop out. WASD move, mouse aim, left click shoot, space/right click dodge-roll.
5. Room cleared: doors open, coins pop out, each player sees their own 3 upgrade cards and picks one.
6. Both stand in the exit, the next room loads. After rooms 1-6, the boss room.
7. Win or lose: back to camp, coins can be spent on meta upgrades, start a new run.

### Controls

|---|---|

| Shoot | Left click (hold for auto-fire) |
| Dodge-roll (short invincibility) | Space or right click |

| Pause overlay | Esc |

### Run & progression
- ✅ Each player picks their own upgrade from their own 3 cards; builds diverge.
- 🔶 Upgrades last for one run only. Example cards: bullets bounce once, +1 max heart, dodge-roll leaves a fire trail, +20% fire rate, piercing shots, a small orbiting shield, heal 2 hearts, revive speed ×2.
- ✅ Meta upgrades stay small. 🔶 About 6 upgrades with 1-3 levels each (e.g. +1 starting heart, slightly faster dodge, one free upgrade reroll per run). Everything is buyable in ~8-10 runs, and all of it together is at most ~20% more power.
- 🔶 Each player keeps their own coins and meta upgrades in a save on their own PC. Coins from a run go in full to both players.

### Look & feel
- ✅ Cute, colourful, light and goofy, pure gameplay.
- ✅ Art: coloured placeholder shapes until M4, then one consistent pixel pack. 🔶 Pack: "0x72 Dungeon Tileset II" from itch.io (16x16, cute dungeon style, check the licence on its page and keep a copy in the repo). No mixing of styles. If the pack lacks a sprite (e.g. a projectile), draw a simple shape in its palette.
- 🔶 Pixel-perfect rendering: low base resolution (e.g. 480x270) scaled up by whole numbers, nearest-neighbour filtering.
- ✅ Hits feel punchy and arcadey: short screen shake, enemies flash white and pop, 30-60 ms hit-pause on kills, knockback, coin pop.
- 🔶 SFX: a free CC0 pack (e.g. Kenney). Licence files go in the repo.

### HUD
Own hearts, coin count, icons of own upgrades, buddy's hearts + an indicator pointing to them when off-screen, "DOWN" marker with the revive progress circle.

## 4b. Rules & edge cases
- **Downed:** at 0 hearts a player goes down and can't act. The buddy revives them by staying within ~1.5 tiles for 3 seconds in total; leaving the radius pauses the progress, taking damage doesn't. Revived with 1 heart. ✅
- **Both down** (including at the same moment) → the run ends, game over screen for both. ✅
- **Room cleared while one is down** → they get up with 1 heart. 🔶
- **No bleed-out timer** in v1; a downed player stays down until revived or the room is cleared. 🔶
- **No friendly fire.** 🔶
- **Upgrade pick:** the exit opens only when both players have picked. No timer. 🔶
- **Next room:** loads when both players stand in the exit zone. 🔶
- **Pause:** Esc opens an overlay for that player only; the game keeps running (it's online). 🔶
- **Client disconnects:** the host keeps playing solo, gets a "your buddy left" message, and the run continues. 🔶
- **Host disconnects:** the client gets "host left" and goes back to the main menu. Coins earned so far in that run are kept. 🔶
- **Join during a run:** not possible in v1; joining only works at the camp/lobby. 🔶
- **Hero choice:** both may pick the same hero. 🔶
- **Who decides what:** the host is the referee for everything shared (enemies, damage to enemies, room state, random rolls including upgrade cards, coins). Each player's own movement, aiming and shooting must feel instant on their own screen, even at 100 ms ping. 🔶
- **Version mismatch:** if host and client builds differ, show "versions don't match" instead of connecting.
