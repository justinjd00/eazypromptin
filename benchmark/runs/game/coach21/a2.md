Before the next phase, one honest note: **online co-op is the biggest risk in this whole project.** It's doable in Godot, and capping it at 2 players makes it a lot more manageable. But it means every system (movement, enemies, damage, upgrades) gets built network-aware from the first milestone, and the first thing we prove is "two PCs in different cities, same room, no desync". It's not a reason to drop it, since playing together is the point. It does mean v1 content stays small.
