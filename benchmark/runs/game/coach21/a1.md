"Dungeon + monsters + getting stronger" fits several quite different games. These are probes to find your direction, not proposals; pick one, mix them, or tell me why none fits:

- **A: Roguelite runs.** 15-20 min runs through random rooms, pick 1 of 3 upgrades after each room, die and start over with a small permanent bonus. *(Hades, Enter the Gungeon, Soul Knight)*
- **B: Loot crawler.** One character you keep, dungeon floors you push deeper into over many sessions, gear with rarities, save games. *(Diablo-lite, Torchlight)* Much more content to make.
- **C: Survivors-like.** You only move, attacks fire on their own, hundreds of monsters, a level-up every 30 seconds, 10-20 min rounds. *(Vampire Survivors, Brotato)* The cheapest of the three to build.
- **Wildcard: Dungeon master.** One friend builds the dungeon and places the monsters, the others try to survive it, then you swap.
