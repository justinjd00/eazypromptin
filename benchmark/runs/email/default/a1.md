Here's a draft you can adapt. I've assumed the heating isn't working properly. Fill in the bracketed parts, and tell me if your situation is different (it's completely out, it's just weak, or you've already reported it once) and I'll adjust the tone.

---

**Subject:** Heating problem at [your address / unit number]

Hi [Landlord's name],

I'm writing to let you know that the heating in my apartment at [address / unit number] isn't working properly. Since [date], [describe the problem: e.g. the radiators stay cold / the heating only runs for a short time / there's no heat in the bedroom and living room]. The indoor temperature has been around [X°] even with the thermostat turned up.

With the colder weather, this is becoming a real problem, so I'd appreciate it if you could arrange for someone to take a look as soon as possible. I'm available [days/times], or I'm happy to coordinate access with the technician directly if that's easier.

Please let me know when someone can come by. Thanks for your help.

Best regards,
[Your name]
[Phone number]

---

A few tips:

- **Put dates and specifics in.** "Since Monday the radiators have been cold" gets acted on faster than "the heating is bad."
- **Keep it in writing.** If you've already mentioned it by phone, add a line like "Following up on our call on [date]" so there's a record.
- **If this is a repeat request,** say so and ask for a specific timeframe ("Could you confirm by [date] when this will be fixed?").
