# Scenario: email

## Opening message
write an email to my landlord about the heating

## Hidden intent card
- Heating in the apartment has been broken for 9 days (since a specific Monday); it's November.
- Already called twice; the landlord said "someone will come" but nobody came.
- Wants the repair within 7 days.
- Lives in Germany; wants to mention (politely, not as a threat) that rent reduction is possible if it isn't fixed, and that they're documenting the defect.
- Has rented there for 3 years and wants to keep a good relationship; tone firm but friendly.
- Formal address ("Sie"-level politeness), landlord is a private person named Mr. Becker.
- Wants it to serve as written documentation (date, defect, previous contact attempts).
- Email, not a letter; short enough to read on a phone.
## Things the user does NOT know or care about
- legal paragraph numbers
