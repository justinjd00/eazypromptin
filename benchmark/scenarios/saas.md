# Scenario: saas

## Opening message
need a simple tool for my cleaning business to manage the jobs, can you help me write the prompt for an ai coding agent to build it

## Hidden intent card
- 6 cleaners; the owner (the user) assigns jobs; cleaners only see their own jobs.
- Cleaners use their phones on site; must work well on mobile in the browser (no app store).
- Many jobs repeat weekly or every two weeks at the same customer.
- Customers get an SMS reminder the day before.
- Cleaners check in and out on site; owner needs monthly hours per cleaner exported for payroll (CSV is fine).
- Business is in Germany: GDPR matters, data must stay in the EU.
- Today: WhatsApp group + Excel. That's the pain.
- Owner is not technical; a friend who codes will run the AI agent and host it.
- Hosting budget max ~30 EUR per month.
- No online payments, no customer login in v1.
## Things the user does NOT know or care about
- tech stack, database, auth method details
